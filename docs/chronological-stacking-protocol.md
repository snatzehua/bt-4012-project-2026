# Chronological score stacking

This separate adaptive study pursues improved positive-class F2 (beta=2), giving missed fraud four times the denominator penalty of false alarms: F2=5TP/(5TP+4FN+FP). It does not optimize support-weighted F1 or impose a 1% review quota or precision floor. All settings are analyst-selected, not group-approved. The two prior dedicated F2 searches failed to improve macro F2 0.3278015781; preserve their negative findings and all four completed studies' identities. Month 7 is already consumed.

The exact finite grid is configs/chronological-stacking.json. Base v2 remains checksum-verified; training and policy selection use months 0-6 only. Reuse the unchanged recipes baseline_balanced, native31, catboost_engineered6 and logistic_engineered from the improvement study. Applicant preprocessing remains model-specific and training-only: original imputation/one-hot for the balanced baseline, numeric NaNs/training categorical levels for native LightGBM, and the existing 65-feature row-local bundle for CatBoost/logistic. Target, month and constant device_fraud_count are excluded from predictors.

For each fixed member, score months 1/2/3 with training strictly before that month. Reuse or refit compatible 4/5/6 predictions and the unweighted baseline on 4/5/6. There are 27 base results, needing 12 early fits and 15 verified reuses with current caches. Absent caches can be refitted into this study's directories; changed caches are rejected. Verify training-row hashes/class counts, source/config/software, score hashes and exact future IDs/labels. Previous output directories are never written.

Each learned preset predicts month 4 using base prediction rows from 1/2/3, month 5 using 1-4, and month 6 using 1-5. Every base score was produced without its own or later month's labels. Combiner preprocessing uses these earlier rows only. Compare four-member stacks and matched balanced-only controls, logit and logit-plus-percentile inputs, C=0.001/0.01/0.1 and positive weights 1/5. The 24 presets produce 72 combiner/control fits. Use training-fitted StandardScaler and L2 LogisticRegression, lbfgs, max_iter=1000, tol=1e-6, seed 2026, at most six threads. Training weights are distinct from evaluation beta and are not estimated bank costs.

Logits clip scores at [1e-6,1-1e-6]. Percentiles use average ties, (rank-.5)/n, within each member's unlabeled prediction-month batch. Feature order is fixed and recorded. The percentile representation requires batch inference; it does not use evaluation labels and does not yield calibrated fraud probabilities. Single-member controls isolate score transformation/calibration effects from combination effects.

Policy evaluation on month 5 learns only from out-of-time combiner predictions from 4; month 6 learns from 4/5. Never optimize thresholds on in-sample combiner outputs. Compare pooled and latest-month maximum-F2 thresholds and learned rank fractions, using the frozen tie/rational-ceiling rules. With 24 learned presets, two old baselines, three policies and two operating months, export 156 forward rows. Select macro F2, then recall, precision and deterministic candidate/policy ID. Reproduce both previous baselines before selection. Report pooled confusion-count F2 separately, and retain failed stack/control, representation/regularization/weight and policy contrasts.

Freeze the selected strategy, its matched single-member control if a four-member stack wins, dependencies, source/config/software/data hashes, feature/scaler/model artifacts and policies. Final combiners train on out-of-time base predictions from 1-6 and are fitted before further month-7 access (at most two final fits). Policies use out-of-time combiner predictions 4/5/6. Record access with the selection digest, then score only selected/control dependencies and both old baselines. All month-7 comparisons remain exploratory on a consumed holdout; no retuning is allowed within this frozen study.

Export F2/F1/F3, precision, recall, AP/ROC, TP/FP/FN/TN, missed fraud, workload, MCC and balanced accuracy. Compare selected versus both baselines and the matched control where applicable. A positive point gain supports only its named exploratory scope, and zero-overlapping intervals preclude clear-gain claims.
