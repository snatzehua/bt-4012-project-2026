## Purpose

Evaluate fixed removal of velocity and local-count feature groups under matched chronological fraud models, native-margin controls and recall-weighted selection, with independently verified and reproducible results.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Prospective isolated preservation and exact individual reuse
The study SHALL declare its complete fixed groups, recipes, domains, arithmetic, policies, factor identities, gates, destinations and winner-dependent transfer before scientific evaluation. It SHALL preserve all nineteen completed studies, separate Newton identities and the stopped attempt, including nineteen identical original/exported selection pairs. Required individual sources SHALL retain exact original populations, preprocessing, recipes, software and saved inference. A wholly absent individual set SHALL reproduce only its exact original recipe in the new destination under unchanged gates; partial, corrupt or incompatible inspected sets SHALL fail without overwrite, fallback or unrelated-grid execution. Original historical manifests SHALL remain unchanged. Historical mutable project-context entries SHALL resolve only to their exact prospectively preserved versions; scientific identities SHALL NOT use that exception.

#### Scenario: Inspect a partial original source
- **WHEN** an inspected required source has any artifact but lacks another required member
- **THEN** execution rejects that set in place without silent fallback or fitting another recipe

#### Scenario: Update mutable context after a frozen run
- **WHEN** current project documentation changes after a historical execution index was written
- **THEN** the original context hashes are verified against exact preserved versions, all scientific entries remain verified at unchanged original paths, and the historical index is not rewritten

### Requirement: Fixed feature removals and matched earlier training
The study SHALL use exactly four representations: all 29 original predictors, removal of the three velocity fields, removal of the three local-count fields, and removal of their union, leaving 29/26/26/23 columns. The declared groups SHALL remain fixed and disjoint; removed fields SHALL NOT reappear through engineered substitutes or missing indicators. Every compared model SHALL use identical strictly earlier ordered training populations/classes, original categorical handling and training-fitted numerical imputation/indicator representation. Original weighted/unweighted full-model settings SHALL remain matched with reduced models, and class costs SHALL be applied once. Current labels, removed values, row order and unrelated future rows SHALL NOT affect a retained row's representation or score.

#### Scenario: Perturb an excluded velocity field
- **WHEN** a velocity-removed model receives different values in the removed original fields while retained data and training identities are unchanged
- **THEN** preprocessing, transformed retained schema and scores remain unchanged without evaluation-time refitting

### Requirement: Actual native margins and valid full-feature controls
Eight pure sources SHALL be finite native model margins: six reduced-feature models and two score-only full-feature controls from original saved baselines. Full controls SHALL NOT be new equivalent-recipe refits or logit conversions of stored probabilities. Actual requested/effective settings, removed/retained schemas, ordered training IDs/classes/costs, fitted rounds/warnings and underlying persisted inference SHALL be independently verified. Native margins SHALL agree with their positive-class probabilities through sigmoid at absolute 1e-12/relative 0 and SHALL NOT be called calibrated fraud probabilities. Native-margin and probability policy differences SHALL remain visible and SHALL NOT be renamed feature-removal gains. Actual full-size rounds SHALL match the declared recipe; any miniature natural termination SHALL be separately recorded as declared.

#### Scenario: Construct a full-feature margin control
- **WHEN** a full-feature control is prepared from a verified original baseline
- **THEN** it uses raw inference from that same saved fitted model and retains original probability comparison evidence without a new fit or guessed score transform

#### Scenario: Encounter invalid persisted inference
- **WHEN** a saved reduced model fails schema, cost, parameter or original numerical persistence checks
- **THEN** execution retains the failure and stops without relaxing recipes or tolerances

New selectable scores SHALL NOT introduce clipping, probability conversion, batch normalization, calibration or learned composition. #### Scenario: Display the pure endpoint

- **THEN** it uses the exact existing pure native-margin identity and values without a refit, probability alias or invented gain
