## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** Test a listwise score-learning surrogate against matched ordinary-loss controls while making query construction, score semantics, chronology and actual-F2 conclusions auditable.

**Non-Goals:** Claim NDCG equals F2, promise a gain or 90% recall, impose a quota/precision floor, change beta, add features/ensembles/calibration, introduce a new dependency, expand the grid after results or regain an independent month-7 holdout.

## Decisions

1. **Separate preserved identity.** Plan configs/direct-ranking.json, docs/direct-ranking-protocol.md, additive grouping/runner/report modules, tests, outputs/baf-direct-ranking-v1/ and reports/baf-direct-ranking-v1/. Extend identity checking from the frozen focal study without modifying any of its three sources or earlier sources. Preserve seven original/exported selection records, prior configurations, all frozen source hashes, Python 3.12.9 and pinned requirements-improvements.txt software. New sources/config are frozen too. Missing reference caches are reproduced in this destination using exact frozen recipes; never rerun an old validate/freeze command. Destination overlap and altered identities fail explicitly. Using a new identity, rather than amending the old search, preserves the negative evidence and consumed-period access history.

2. **Data and native inputs.** Use only verified BAF Base v2, SHA-256 7bf10a37ce07e72e14c1b09e5efee3d27261baff4facc7da767b0474dcf9b809. Predictions for months 4/5/6 train on 0-3/0-4/0-5. Use 29 original predictors, excluding fraud_bool, month and constant device_fraud_count. Reuse frozen native LightGBM handling: numeric NaNs, training-learned categorical levels and unseen levels missing. Fit preprocessing before reordering eligible training rows. Neither month nor stable IDs become predictors. The old balanced/unweighted LightGBM and native31 references retain their original, model-specific preprocessing, which is not claimed identical to the new matched inputs. No random-split drift claim is added.

3. **Label-independent grouping with complete coverage.** Sort eligible month values; for each month, start from its ascending stable row IDs and apply np.random.default_rng(np.random.SeedSequence([2026, month])).permutation. Concatenate these per-month permutations. This ordered training ID vector is identical for both query sizes and ordinary controls. Within each month, split the permutation into contiguous groups of the declared size; retain a smaller final group of at least two rows. If its tail would contain one row, merge it with the preceding group, producing size+1. A month of 2 through size rows is one group; fewer than two eligible rows fails explicitly rather than dropping a row. Queries never cross months. The grouping routine accepts eligible IDs/months/size/seed, not labels. Descriptive per-query fraud counts are joined only after membership is fixed.

   Preserve the ordered-ID hash, group-boundary hash/vector, per-query month/size/fraud counts, zero-positive/all-positive fractions and total row coverage. Keep all-negative groups rather than removing or repairing them using labels. Fixtures must verify unique complete coverage, deterministic grouping, month boundaries, singleton tails and unchanged membership after arbitrary label permutation. These artificial queries approximate local ranking sets; they are not real sessions or investigator batches. Unlike grouping all applications into one enormous query, finite groups bound computation and allow the declared size comparison.

4. **Finite grid and matched controls.** Four rankers cross group size {256,1024} with reg_lambda {0.1,1.0}. Use installed LightGBM 4.7.0 LGBMRanker objective rank_xendcg, integer relevance fraud_bool in {0,1} and objective_seed=2026. Two binary logistic controls cross the same two lambda values, with identical ordered training IDs and no query-dependent parameter, so a duplicate control for each group size is unnecessary. Both use native inputs, no sample/class weights, no scale_pos_weight adjustment and zero initialization (binary boost_from_average=False; verify ranker's zero initial margin in a controlled integration fixture).

   Fix all six presets at n_estimators=800, learning_rate=.03, num_leaves=31, min_child_samples=100, min_child_weight=.001, max_bin=255, colsample_bytree=.9, subsample=1, subsample_freq=0, deterministic=True, force_col_wise=True, seed2026 and at most six threads. No early stopping or outcome-driven adjustment. The two lambda settings are a small predeclared regularization comparison because objective gradient/Hessian scales differ; equal lambda does not make their effective regularization identical. No LambdaRank sweep is included: this study tests one listwise objective without adding its pair/truncation choices. Six presets times three months are **18 new development fits**; three references times three months are **nine reference results**, giving **27 prediction results**. Any tiny integration fits are counted separately, not as full development fits.

5. **Installed integration and raw-score inference.** Check official/installed group semantics: positive contiguous group counts must sum exactly to training size. Verify small actual fits with valid integer labels, finite raw predictions, deterministic seeds/order, saved-booster reconstruction and training-only categories before full fitting. Reject mismatched group coverage and invalid labels explicitly. Save booster, adapter, preset, full parameter identities and training/group metadata; predictions retain stable future IDs/labels, raw score and hashes. Capture fit seconds, warnings and actual fitted trees rather than assuming every attempted round adds a tree.

   Use finite raw predictions directly for all new empirical thresholds, ranking, AP and ROC metrics. Binary controls also expose raw margins, so new comparisons avoid mixed inference transformations. Old reference scores can remain on their original scales: every threshold is fitted within its own candidate and no cross-model score averaging occurs. Do not apply expit or clip ranking scores to [0,1], which could collapse extreme ranks. Reuse finite-score empirical policies and confusion metrics, avoiding probability-validated reporting helpers. Do not report Brier/log loss or interpret a ranking score as fraud probability. Test scores outside [0,1], ties, monotone-transform rank agreement on a fixture and cache/identity tampering.

No preset uses the current scored month's labels for model or policy fitting. Nine models times three policies times two forward months yield **54 operating rows and 27 macro summaries**. Select actual macro month-5/6 F2, then recall, precision, candidate/policy ID. Reproduce both old pooled benchmarks before freeze.

   Export every monthly row and macro summary, and pooled confusion-count F2 separately. Compare each ranker with the binary control at its lambda under the same policy; compare query sizes at fixed lambda/policy and lambdas at fixed size/policy. Export all candidates versus both old pooled references and policy-only differences against their own pooled rule. Retain unsuccessful contrasts. Report F1/F2/F3, precision/recall, MCC/balanced accuracy, TP/FP/FN/TN, missed fraud, legitimate flags, review fraction, AP/ROC, runtime, warning and grouping diagnostics. Ranking surrogate/NDCG or AP gains cannot replace actual F2. Group size changes loss construction and gradient scale as well as ranking comparisons; causal claims must remain specific to tested presets.

7. **Freeze matched dependencies and exploratory transfer.** Freeze the actual winner and its learned policy, selected model's pooled comparator, both old pooled baselines and applicable matched controls with all sources/config/software/model/prediction/group identities before further month-7 access. If a ranker wins, include its same-lambda binary control under the selected policy. 8. **Conditional uncertainty and goal fidelity.** Use 500 paired fixed-decision row bootstrap draws, seed1027, for selected versus both old references, same-model pooled and any matched controls. Use identical sampled rows within each comparison and retain draws. Test unequal-month aggregation against explicit manual resampling. These intervals exclude adaptive selection, retraining, temporal/generative uncertainty and multiplicity adjustment. A positive point difference supports only a measured gain on its named scope; overlap with zero forbids a clear/confirmed gain claim. Do not silently add a significance gate to the user's objective, and do not treat a development gain failing consumed-period transfer as out-of-time improvement. ## Risks / Trade-offs

- Artificial queries may not represent fraud decisions or yield useful ranking signal -> label-independent membership, query-size comparison, retained all-negative groups, matched ordinary controls and actual forward F2.
- Ranking scores may shift across months and harm fixed thresholds -> keep both earlier empirical threshold policies and the learned rank-fraction rule, reporting failures without month-7 recalibration.
- Objective scale interacts with regularization and grouping -> finite lambda/grid contrasts and restricted causal interpretation.
- Repeated development reuse can overfit the search -> retain all seven previous negative findings, predeclare finite presets, disclose adaptation and avoid fresh-holdout claims.
- Native model APIs can obscure query/inference semantics -> installed miniature integration and saved-model/raw-score audits before full fits.
- Checkpoint identity changes could corrupt reproduction -> reject partial/incompatible caches, preserve old destinations and freeze additive source/config identity.

## Migration Plan

During apply, create the new config/protocol and additive modules; verify grouping and installed integration before full fitting; fit/audit the finite development grid; reconstruct actual metrics; freeze selection/control dependencies; transfer only those frozen dependencies; export uncertainty/findings; inspect figures and preserve identities; update reproduction/context; sync verified requirements and archive. New outputs are additive and isolated from old studies.

## Primary sources

- [LightGBM objective parameters](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/en/stable/Parameters.html): rank_xendcg uses integer relevance labels and an objective seed. Its retrieval-oriented ranking objective is not the project's F2 target.
- [LightGBM ranker API](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/en/stable/pythonapi/lightgbm.LGBMRanker.html): group sizes describe contiguous training queries and sum to the number of samples. Inference does not require future labels or query-dependent threshold fitting.
- [Bruch, An Alternative Cross Entropy Loss for Learning-to-Rank](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.09798): motivates a listwise NDCG surrogate; retrieval benchmarks do not establish BAF performance.
- [Pinned LightGBM 4.7.0 ranking objective source](https://github.com/microsoft/LightGBM/blob/v4.7.0/src/objective/rank_objective.hpp): supports auditing implementation semantics, including objective randomness and singleton handling. This study's grouping/grid are explicit adaptations, not claims prescribed by the paper.
