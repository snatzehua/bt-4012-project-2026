## Why

Completed follow-up searches still leave the balanced baseline's macro chronological F2 of 0.327802 unbeaten; the latest focal-loss study's best focal policy scored 0.323007. Test whether learning a relative fraud ranking, rather than another pointwise classification loss or cutoff adjustment, improves actual forward F2.

This serves the user's recall-weighted performance goal and the revised BAF proposal's model/class-imbalance and threshold questions. Ranking loss is a surrogate, not F2, and its information-retrieval results do not establish a fraud-data gain.

## What Changes

- Declare a separate finite direct-ranking study, preserving all seven completed studies and their negative evidence, with beta=2 and no review quota or precision floor.
- Compare four listwise ranking presets with two ordinary binary-logistic controls using identical native inputs, chronological training rows, deterministic row order and matched tree/regularization settings.
- Form artificial training queries independently of labels within training months; audit group coverage, all-negative groups, singleton handling and seed/identity preservation. These groups are an optimization device, not observed bank sessions.
- Keep finite raw ranking scores as scores, avoiding probability or calibration claims. Learn the existing three earlier-only empirical operating policies and select actual macro month-5/6 F2 against the old balanced/unweighted references.
- Freeze selected/control recipes before further consumed-month transfer and export checked predictions, contrasts, workload, conditional uncertainty, negative findings and reproducibility evidence.

None. Existing requirements, source/config/software identities, selections and results remain frozen.
