## Why

The completed Newton study improved all 18 paired spline-versus-linear comparisons but remained below the existing fusion; this change asks whether its smooth additive scores provide complementary information that improves actual chronological F2 when combined with that fusion. It continues the revised BAF proposal's model/imbalance experiments and lecturer feedback on justified model-specific features, without assuming blending will improve detection.

## What Changes

- Declare a separate finite common-log-odds fusion study using the four existing C=0.1 Newton recipes (linear/spline, unweighted/balanced), all three pooled references and fixed additive-member weights 0.1/0.25/0.5.
- Reuse the checked fitted development models. Preserve all eleven completed studies, both Newton fit/analysis identities and the stopped L-BFGS attempt. C=0.1 is an adaptive choice from the completed development evidence, not an approved group setting or fresh confirmation.
- Compare 23 strategies under three earlier-only policies: original/transformed Newton singles, three references and twelve fixed blends. Retain actual F2 beta=2 without quota/floor, complete matched comparisons, numerical/provenance checks and bounded frozen transfer.
- Export 138 forward rows, 69 macro/pooled summaries and 433 contrasts, with clipping and declared score/error-overlap diagnostics. Local development expects 21 base reuses and zero new fits; genuinely absent required caches use exact frozen recipes in the new destination with explicit fit counts.
- Core work is this fixed composition question. Additional C/feature/solver searches, learned blend weights, batch-dependent scores, new calibration and losing-setting month-7 sweeps are excluded. Transfer remains exploratory on an already-consumed month.
