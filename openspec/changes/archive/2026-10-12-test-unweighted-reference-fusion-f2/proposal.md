## Why

The completed fixed interaction-score fusion study improved macro development F2 to 0.330793 but its frozen policy scored 0.371534 on consumed month 7, below unweighted LightGBM's 0.376884. This separate exploratory study tests whether that reference contributes useful complementary scores to existing tree/spline margins, with matched balanced-addition controls, continuing the revised BAF proposal's model/imbalance investigation and the lecturer's request for model-specific justification; no gain is assumed.
