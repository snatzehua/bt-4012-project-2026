## Purpose

Test whether fixed balanced or unweighted fraud-score additions complement preserved tree/spline margins under comparable chronological recall-weighted evaluation and independently verified exploratory transfer.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Separate finite preserved addition study
The study SHALL declare every source, anchor, addition weight, strategy identity, policy, reference, gate and destination before new score evaluation. It SHALL preserve all fourteen completed identities, separate Newton fit/analysis histories and the stopped attempt. Required individual reuse SHALL verify frozen recipes, training populations, preprocessing, software, numerical validity and serialized inference. Wholly absent required caches SHALL reproduce only their exact individual recipes in the new destination; partial/incompatible caches SHALL fail without overwrite or silent fallback. Unrelated historical grids SHALL NOT be required or rerun.

#### Scenario: Reproduce without ignored required caches
- **WHEN** mandatory ancestry and fourteen original/exported selection records are intact but required caches are wholly absent
- **THEN** only the declared individual recipes are reproduced, actual fits are counted and historical studies remain unchanged

#### Scenario: Encounter an incomplete required origin
- **WHEN** an inspected required model, adapter, score or provenance set is partial or incompatible
- **THEN** that origin is rejected without overwriting it or advancing to another recipe

### Requirement: Fixed domain-correct label-free margin addition
Every added probability-like score SHALL be validated and transformed using the declared clipping rule before a fixed weighted sum with the preserved finite margin. An anchor margin SHALL NOT receive a probability logit or be required to lie between zero and one. Score arithmetic SHALL reject invalid domains, dimensions, alignment, weights and non-finite values. Current labels, unrelated rows, batch statistics and learned weights SHALL NOT change a row's composed score. Added scores and margins SHALL NOT be asserted calibrated probabilities or operational cost estimates.

#### Scenario: Add a valid score to an unbounded anchor
- **WHEN** a valid anchor lies outside the probability range
- **THEN** the anchor is retained as a finite margin and only the added probability-like member is transformed before the declared weighted sum

#### Scenario: Change labels or unrelated applications
- **WHEN** one row's source scores remain fixed while aligned labels or unrelated applications change
- **THEN** that row's composed score remains unchanged

### Requirement: Canonical historical identity and reference precedence
Any declared addition algebraically identical to an existing strategy SHALL retain that strategy's historical identity and reconstructible equivalence; it SHALL NOT become a renamed extra strategy. Original reference identities SHALL retain their fixed reference policies and reference-winner transfer precedence. Visible role aliases SHALL share identical work without being removed from comparisons. Displayed endpoint controls SHALL use existing declared strategies rather than introduce new evaluated settings.

#### Scenario: Recover the existing asymmetric tree blend
- **WHEN** balanced LightGBM receives half the total weight in an addition to the equal-weight tree margin
- **THEN** the resulting quarter-XGBoost/three-quarter-balanced margin uses its existing historical strategy identity and cannot establish a gain by renaming

### Requirement: Comparable chronological actual-F2 selection
All declared strategies SHALL predict identical future applications from models and preprocessing fitted on earlier months. Operating policies SHALL learn only from eligible earlier out-of-time predictions and labels. Selection SHALL use actual macro forward positive-class F2 beta=2 with deterministic ties and no review quota or precision floor. The latest completed winner and all four earlier fixed reference policies SHALL remain visible. AP, pooled F2, diversity or consumed-month results SHALL NOT replace the development objective or select a different winner.

#### Scenario: Decide for the next development month
- **WHEN** a strategy produces month-6 decisions
- **THEN** its models/preprocessing use months 0-5, its policy uses only eligible month-4/5 predictions and labels, and score composition uses no month-6 labels

### Requirement: Complete matched effects and diagnostic evidence
The study SHALL retain every declared fixed-reference, own-policy, anchor/member, added-weighting, addition-weight and otherwise-matched anchor-representation comparison with reconstructible factors and arithmetic, including ties and negative effects. Native/logit controls, clipping, diversity, original-feature, pair-feature, coefficient, numerical, resource and provenance evidence SHALL accompany the results. Comparisons changing multiple anchor factors SHALL NOT be described as isolating one factor. Diagnostics SHALL remain descriptive and SHALL NOT change the finite search or selection metric.

#### Scenario: Attribute an unweighted-addition gain
- **WHEN** an unweighted addition is reported as improving a comparator
- **THEN** the balanced addition at the same anchor, weight and policy remains available, along with its native/logit member, unchanged anchor and other declared weights

### Requirement: Frozen bounded exploratory transfer and honest acceptance
Before further consumed-month access, the actual winner, five fixed reference policies, same-strategy pooled rule and predetermined winner-type controls SHALL be frozen with exact dependencies, earlier-only rules and cache/type-specific fit bounds. Only the declared seven/eight/nine/fourteen/fifteen roles SHALL transfer with deduplicated work and actual fit/reuse counts. Losing-grid sweeps, hindsight cutoffs and independent-confirmation claims SHALL be excluded. Broader goal achievement SHALL require positive measured F2 differences against all five references on both development and consumed transfer; clear-gain language SHALL additionally require positive conditional interval lower bounds on the named scope.

#### Scenario: Transfer a new addition winner
- **WHEN** a nonreference addition wins development selection
- **THEN** its predetermined fourteen or fifteen roles and exact dependencies are frozen before access, and alternate addition weights reuse the same source models

#### Scenario: Fail any required reference comparison
- **WHEN** the frozen selected policy fails a required development or consumed comparison
- **THEN** the findings retain that failure and the broader performance goal remains unachieved

### Requirement: Verified reproducible completion and retained gate failures
Completion SHALL require checked prospective declarations, complete outputs/provenance, independently reconstructed source features/inference/numerical validity, score compositions, earlier-only policies, metrics, contrast factors, winner and conditional paired uncertainty, inspected figures, executable reproduction instructions and assistance attribution. Required reproductions SHALL retain frozen numerical/resource gates without alternative recipes or solver fallback; failed gates SHALL retain evidence and prevent completed-study status. Adaptive anchor/weight choices, repeated search and consumed-holdout limitations SHALL be explicit. Planning or finite-search completion SHALL NOT establish a performance gain.

#### Scenario: Fail an exact required reproduction
- **WHEN** a required source reproduction fails its frozen numerical or allocation gate
- **THEN** completed work and failure evidence are preserved, with no post-outcome recipe relaxation or completion claim

#### Scenario: Complete the finite experiment
- **WHEN** the study is declared complete
- **THEN** every requirement has checked output evidence, preservation/tests/strict OpenSpec checks pass and goal status follows measured five-reference comparisons
