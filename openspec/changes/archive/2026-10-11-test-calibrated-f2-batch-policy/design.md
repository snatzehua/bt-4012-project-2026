## Context

See proposal.md for motivation. The completed stacking study raised macro AP from 0.1832 to 0.1894 for its best F2 stack, but that stack's macro F2 was 0.3268, below balanced LightGBM 0.3278015781. Its month-5/6 AP gains occurred in both folds. This motivates testing operating-rule transfer; it does not prove that threshold selection is the cause of failure.

Five studies are frozen, including configs/chronological-stacking.json and baf/stacking*.py. All new code and outputs must be additive. Base v2, months 0-6 development and consumed month 7 remain unchanged. Both old reference policies must remain visible: balanced pooled F2 selected on development, and the stronger observed unweighted policy on consumed month 7. Research settings below are analyst-selected, not group-approved.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** Test a smoother, probability-based batch operating rule with fixed model rankings, isolate the effect from empirical threshold transfer, and explain calibration/ranking/decision limits using verified actual F2.

**Non-Goals:** Change beta or the achieved-performance definition, impose a 1% quota or precision floor, invent a bank cost ratio, optimize on month-7 labels, claim exact expected-F2 optimization, add a new model search, or report label-aware hindsight as a usable policy.

## Decisions

1. **Three fixed score sources.** Reuse baseline_balanced, baseline_lgbm and stack_logit_percentile_c0p01_w1 from the completed stacking study. The third is its best four-member stack by development F2; its fixed members, StandardScaler, representation, C=0.01 and positive weight=1 remain unchanged. Base models retain their frozen applicant features and training-only preprocessing. Reuse months 4/5/6 with exact IDs/labels, training histories, source/model/prediction hashes and source/config/software/data identities checked. With current caches this gives nine verified source predictions and no new base/combiner development fit. Choosing only fixed representatives limits another broad adaptive model search. Their selection here is explicitly informed by earlier development results.

2. **Reproducible absent-cache route.** Never require ignored caches to exist for a fresh clone. Reproduce missing base/stack predictions using the frozen recipes in new directories. If all caches are absent, the necessary dependencies are the four stack members on months 1-6, the unweighted reference on 4-6 (27 base results) and the single fixed stack on 4/5/6 (three chronological combiner fits). Reuse verified dependencies where available, and record actual fits/reuses separately from the nine score-source results. Do not call an old frozen validate/freeze command or write its destination. Preserve every prior selection and source identity. This is reproduction of existing recipes, not an additional hyperparameter search.

3. **Finite calibration grid and chronology.** For each score source and evaluation month 5/6, fit two calibration methods with two histories: pooled eligible months versus the latest eligible month. Month 5 uses source predictions from 4; month 6 pooled uses 4/5 and latest uses 5. The underlying predictions are out of time; stack predictions also have earlier combiner training. There are 3 sources × 2 methods × 2 histories × 2 evaluation months = 24 development calibrator fits. Even when pooled/latest training populations coincide on month 5, retain both declared policy rows and their provenance. No current-month labels fit calibration or determine the flagged count.

4. **Calibrators.** Sigmoid-logit calibration uses fixed clipping [1e-6,1-1e-6], logit scores, training-only StandardScaler and unweighted LogisticRegression(C=1e6, lbfgs, max_iter=1000, tol=1e-8, seed 2026). It is deliberately a one-dimensional probability fit without positive class reweighting. Require a nonnegative fitted slope so this stays a monotone calibration; reject an incompatible fit rather than silently reverse the ranking. Isotonic uses increasing=True and out_of_bounds='clip' on raw source scores. Empty/one-class training is handled explicitly: empty inputs are rejected, and a one-class population produces its constant observed class probability. No calibration hyperparameter search is included. Record warnings and refuse unresolved convergence issues. These fitted estimates are not assumed calibrated under temporal drift; forward Brier/log-loss and reliability summaries test that assumption.

5. **Plug-in batch F2 rule.** For actual decisions selecting k cases, F2=5TP/(4P+k), where P is the total actual fraud count. For an unlabeled batch with calibrated probabilities p_i, substitute estimated counts to define S(k)=5*sum(top-k p_i)/(4*sum(all p_i)+k). Enumerate k=0..n, choose the largest S with the smallest k in a tie, and flag that prefix. For k=0 define S=0; all-zero probabilities therefore flag none. Rank by calibrated probability descending, then raw source score descending, then stable row ID ascending. The raw score resolves flat isotonic blocks without current labels, preserving the original ranking under monotone calibration. Record the chosen k, predicted total positives, estimated TP and surrogate value. Selection uses the full current unlabeled batch and must be described as batch inference.

   This is a ratio of estimated counts, not E[5TP/(4P+k)] in general. No claim of exact expected F2 or Bayes optimality is made. Sorting plus cumulative sums gives a practical O(n log n) decision rule for roughly 100k cases; the exact expected-utility algorithms and their distribution assumptions are outside this study. The surrogate is an operating-policy hypothesis; only actual evaluated F2 can establish a measured gain.

6. **Empirical controls and selection.** Each fixed score source also retains the existing pooled maximum-F2 threshold, latest-month maximum-F2 threshold and learned rank fraction, fitted on earlier out-of-time predictions with the unchanged tie/rounding rules. The four calibrated batch policies plus three empirical controls produce 3 × 7 × 2 = 42 forward operating rows. Source AP/ranking remains a diagnostic; it is not the objective. Select actual macro month-5/6 F2, then recall, precision and deterministic model/policy ID. Reproduce old balanced/unweighted pooled macro F2 0.3278015781 and 0.3226536849. Export policy-versus-same-model-pooled contrasts and both old baselines on identical cases, plus pooled confusion-count F2. A surrogate, Brier or AP gain is not a substitute for an F2 gain.

7. **Freeze, diagnostics and exploratory transfer.** Freeze the actual development selection, policy parameters or final calibrator artifact, score-model recipe/dependencies, source/config/software/data identity and deterministic batch rule before further month-7 access. Final calibration uses eligible 4/5/6 source predictions for pooled or 6 for latest. If the fixed stack is selected, fit its final combiner on the frozen out-of-time base predictions 1-6 in the new destination before further access (at most one final combiner fit). Never modify the old stack study. Further transfer scores only selected model dependencies, both raw old baselines and the selected model's empirical pooled comparator. Any selected calibrated method has only its one frozen final calibrator; losing calibrated policies are not swept on month 7. All consumed-period observations are exploratory and cannot retune the frozen rule.

   After development selection is frozen, export a label-aware best deterministic ranking-prefix F2 for each of the three source models on months 5/6 (six diagnostic rows). This is an optimistic bound for that fixed ranking, not a general model ceiling or deployable policy. Store its diagnostic label and generation ordering; exclude it from selection and policy fitting. Do not compute a month-7 hindsight optimum. Its gap from actual frozen decisions helps interpret ranking-versus-policy limitations without crediting hindsight as improvement.

8. **Evidence and verification.** Export all 42 forward rows and failures, calibration source months/artifacts, chosen k and surrogate quantities, raw/calibrated Brier/log-loss and ten equal-frequency score-bin reliability summaries, actual F2/F1/F3/precision/recall/AP/ROC, TP/FP/FN/TN, workload, MCC and balanced accuracy. Recompute decisions and metrics directly from source scores, frozen calibrators and IDs. Test the prefix surrogate against brute-force tiny examples, monotone/flat ties, clipping, empty/one-class inputs, all-zero/all-one probabilities, calibration/scaling chronology, label-independent batch choices, identical populations, rejected altered caches and reproducible new-directory refits. Verify the label-aware diagnostic is generated after freeze and cannot enter selection inputs.

   Use 500 paired row-bootstrap draws at fixed realized decisions, seed 1027, with within-month resampling for development macro differences. Compare selected against both old baselines and the same-model empirical pooled comparator. Conditional intervals exclude recalibration/retraining, selection, between-month/generative uncertainty and multiplicity adjustment; they do not recompute a new unlabeled-batch policy inside each draw. Overlapping zero precludes clear-gain claims. Development gains that fail consumed-period transfer must be stated, and a finished search without a measured gain leaves the objective active. Inspect calibration/operating figures and preserve all five earlier studies before/after execution.

9. **Artifacts and reproduction.** Add configs/calibrated-f2-policy.json, docs/calibrated-f2-policy-protocol.md, additive baf modules/tests, ignored outputs/baf-calibrated-f2-policy-v1/ and checked reports/baf-calibrated-f2-policy-v1/. Use the existing pinned improvement environment, with no new dependency. README must include Base access, all five small frozen-record restoration steps, missing-cache reproduction and sequential validation/freeze/diagnostic/transfer/report commands. Update AGENTS, context and assistance acknowledgment. Source-model identities and fallback fit counts must be explicit so a fresh clone does not silently rely on ignored prediction files.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Calibration can drift or be misspecified → measure forward reliability/log-loss and actual F2; never equate the surrogate with achieved performance.
- A more accurate ranker can still have an unsuitable operating point → keep same-model empirical controls and the isolated post-freeze hindsight bound.
- Isotonic creates flat score blocks → deterministic raw-score/ID tie resolution and explicit tie tests.
- More adaptive selection can find noise → finite declared grid, retained earlier failures and conditional uncertainty limits.
- Batch probabilities can overestimate fraud and increase workload → disclose estimated versus observed prevalence and actual FP/FN/flagged counts, with no unsupported cost claim.
- Missing source caches require extra compute → exact frozen recipes, explicit fallback counts, checkpoints and new destinations.

## Migration Plan

Declare the new finite config/protocol and preservation identities; implement score-source reproduction, earlier-only calibrators and the label-free batch rule; run and verify 24 calibrator fits/42 forward rows; freeze; generate isolated development hindsight diagnostics; transfer only frozen selected dependencies; audit/inspect/export; update reproduction/context/assistance; synchronize implemented requirements and archive after checked completion. Previous studies remain recoverable and unchanged if new outputs are removed.

## Method references

The [scikit-learn calibration guide](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html) describes sigmoid/isotonic calibration and the need for calibration data disjoint from model fitting. [Ye et al., ICML 2012](https://icml.cc/2012/papers/175.pdf) distinguish empirical F-measure optimization from probability-based expected-utility decisions and discuss sensitivity to probability-model accuracy. This study tests a count plug-in approximation, not their exact expected-utility algorithm; their i.i.d. results do not establish performance under BAF temporal drift.
