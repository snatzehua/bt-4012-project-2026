## Context

See proposal.md for motivation. Six studies are frozen. The calibration study's best calibrated macro F2 is 0.3271496689 versus baseline 0.3278015781; label-aware fixed-ranking optima average 0.328776 for balanced and 0.328951 for the stack. These post-freeze diagnostics are optimistic development observations, not deployable improvements or general model ceilings. They motivate a different score-learning objective rather than prove its benefit. Month 7 remains consumed. All settings below are analyst-selected and adaptive, not approved group decisions.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** Test whether focusing score learning on hard examples improves actual chronological F2, while separating ordinary-loss implementation differences and numerical safeguards from focal-loss comparisons.

**Non-Goals:** Promise a gain or 90% recall, impose a review quota/precision floor, change beta, invent costs, retune on month 7, add another feature/architecture search, or treat focal scores as calibrated probabilities.

Use the existing pinned environment, Python 3.12.9 and at most six threads; no new dependency. Reproduce absent reference scores in new directories using frozen recipes, never old validate/freeze commands. Preserve earlier unsuccessful evidence.

2. **Same data and inputs.** Use the verified Base v2 checksum and strictly earlier training months for predictions 4/5/6: 0-3, 0-4 and 0-5. Retain 29 original predictors, excluding fraud_bool/month/constant device_fraud_count; use the frozen native LightGBM adapter with numeric NaNs and training-learned categorical levels, unseen levels missing. No engineered features or label-derived feature selection is added. References are old baseline_balanced, baseline_lgbm and native31, all on identical future rows. 3. **Finite matched grid.** Four focal presets cross gamma {1,2} with positive training weight {1,3}; negatives have weight 1. Two custom gamma=0 presets are ordinary weighted logistic loss. Two built-in binary-logistic controls use scale_pos_weight {1,3}. All eight use n_estimators=800, learning_rate=.03, num_leaves=31, min_child_samples=100, reg_lambda=5, colsample_bytree=.9, deterministic=True, force_col_wise=True, seed 2026, and boost_from_average=False. No validation early stopping. All start at raw margin zero; do not inherit a different class-prior offset for one loss. Do not apply additional class_weight/sample_weight or scale_pos_weight on custom objectives. This is 8 × 3 = 24 new development fits plus 3 × 3 = 9 reference results. Training weights are preferences, not evaluation beta or cost estimates. Ordinary controls use the same initialization, unlike the separately preserved old native31 reference.

4. **Loss and derivatives.** For y in {0,1}, t=2y-1, q=sigmoid(t*z), r=1-q and a=weight(y), define L=-a*r^gamma*log(q). Its raw-margin derivatives are g=a*t*r^gamma*(gamma*q*log(q)-r) and h=a*r^gamma*q*(gamma*log(q)*(r-gamma*q)+(2*gamma+1)*r). Evaluate q and r with stable expit(t*z)/expit(-t*z), and log(q)=-logaddexp(0,-t*z), avoiding log(0) and cancellation at extreme margins. The formulas are an explicit algebraic derivation of this declared weighted focal objective. At gamma=0 they reduce to weighted logistic derivatives; do not substitute clipped derivatives for the mathematical finite-difference checks.

   Focal loss can have negative curvature. For gamma>0, supply max(h,1e-6) to LightGBM as an explicitly approximate diagonal curvature; leave the gradient unchanged. Log per-iteration replaced-curvature counts/fractions and extrema, not per-row arrays. Gamma=0 supplies its actual nonnegative Hessian. This safeguard changes optimizer behavior and is not exact Newton optimization. Validate true derivatives against central finite differences at moderate margins (both labels, gamma 0/1/2 and weights 1/3), test negative-curvature cases and floor statistics, and verify finite outputs at margins ±1000. Invalid/nonfinite inputs fail explicitly.

5. **Ordinary-loss integration gate and inference.** Use the installed LightGBM custom-objective contract: y_pred contains raw margins, and the callable returns grad/hess. Always obtain raw predictions with booster_.predict(raw_score=True) and transform once with expit; never assume predict_proba on a custom objective returns probabilities. Save the booster, adapter, recipe and numeric trace; verify saved-model predictions reconstruct. Test gamma=0 derivatives against ordinary logistic loss, and compare custom versus built-in scores on a fixed well-conditioned miniature dataset with matched parameters and zero initialization, max absolute probability difference <=1e-6. Gate interpretation on these checks. For full fits export custom-minus-built-in raw/probability score discrepancies, rank agreement and actual policy differences; numerical split divergence is diagnostic, not evidence focal loss works. Do not assert bitwise equivalence after hundreds of boosting rounds. If a miniature gate fails, inspect/fix the integration before production comparisons, preserving any completed study identity.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Hard examples may be label noise or unobservable fraud → retain ordinary controls and actual forward F2; do not assume focusing them helps.
- Nonconvex curvature and loss-scale changes affect boosting → log floors and compare matched controls; disclose approximate optimization and regularization interactions.
- Custom-objective inference can silently use raw margins as probabilities → explicit raw prediction plus one expit and integration tests.
- Reused development data amplifies adaptive selection → finite grid, visible negative results and conditional uncertainty caveats.
- New fitting costs time → sequential checkpoints, six-thread limit and verified reuse; no expansion after inspecting results under this identity.

## Migration Plan

All outputs are additive, so removing this study's new destination leaves prior frozen work intact.
