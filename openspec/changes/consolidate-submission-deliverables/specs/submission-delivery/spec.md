## Purpose

Provide reproducible evidence exports and traceable academic report and presentation drafts without altering frozen scientific studies.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Traceable submission evidence
The submission workflow SHALL export measured source rows and SHA-256 provenance without fitting models or mutating frozen study files.

#### Scenario: Export verified findings
- **WHEN** the submission evidence command runs
- **THEN** its output identifies source paths and hashes, preserves evaluation scope and rejects inconsistent confusion counts.

### Requirement: Evidence-linked report and presentation
The delivery SHALL include an 8–12 main-page PDF report, editable report source and editable PPTX skeleton that distinguish exploratory consumed-month results, report actual policies and acknowledge assistance.

#### Scenario: Inspect submission drafts
- **WHEN** a group member opens the deliverables
- **THEN** the report follows the required course structure and slides contain evidence-linked content, timing and explicit unresolved group metadata.

### Requirement: Honest reproduction and handoff
The workflow SHALL document export versus model retraining commands and outstanding human steps without claiming unfinished experiments or unavailable publication succeeded.

#### Scenario: Prepare final handoff
- **WHEN** artifacts have been checked
- **THEN** verification identifies executed checks and leaves filenames, names, video and teaching-team repository access as explicit checks if unresolved.
