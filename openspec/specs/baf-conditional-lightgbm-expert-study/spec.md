# baf-conditional-lightgbm-expert-study Specification

## Purpose

Evaluate fixed group-conditioned fraud rankings with global fallbacks, comparable controls and chronological recall-weighted selection, preserving independent verification and honest exploratory interpretation.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Prospective isolated preserved study
The study SHALL declare its finite populations, eligibility, sources, score arithmetic, policies, controls, destinations, verification gates and acceptance criteria before new evaluation. It SHALL preserve all seventeen completed studies, separate Newton fit/analysis identities and the stopped attempt. Reuse SHALL independently verify exact recipes, earlier training populations, train-only preprocessing, software, numerical validity and saved inference. Only required individual sources SHALL be inspected. Wholly absent required sources SHALL reproduce their exact individual recipes in the new destination under unchanged gates; partial, corrupt or incompatible inspected sets SHALL fail without overwrite or silent fallback. Unrelated historical grids SHALL NOT be required.

#### Scenario: Encounter a partial required source
- **WHEN** an inspected required cache lacks any member of its declared model, prediction or provenance set
- **THEN** the study rejects it and preserves existing evidence without silently trying another source

#### Scenario: Reproduce a wholly absent required source
- **WHEN** every file of a required cache is absent while mandatory ancestry remains intact
- **THEN** only its exact individual recipe is reproduced in the new destination and actual fits are counted separately

### Requirement: Earlier-only conditional populations and global fallback
Conditional models SHALL use fixed payment-type or housing-status memberships with eligibility determined exclusively from earlier training rows and labels. Eligible levels SHALL contain at least 10000 rows, 100 fraud and 100 legitimate cases. Sparse, absent, missing and unseen memberships SHALL use the original same-weight global model. Failed eligible fits SHALL fail rather than become fallback decisions. Every application SHALL receive exactly one aligned score; subset predictions SHALL retain explicit member identities and SHALL NOT be represented as comparable full-population results. Current labels, row order and unrelated applications SHALL NOT affect routing or scores.

#### Scenario: Predict an unseen future membership
- **WHEN** an application has a partition value absent from the fixed eligible membership set
- **THEN** it receives the same-weight global prediction and an explicit missing, unseen or ineligible fallback reason without a new expert fit

#### Scenario: Fail an eligible expert
- **WHEN** a population meets training eligibility but its model fails a numerical or persistence gate
- **THEN** execution stops with retained failure evidence rather than substituting global predictions

### Requirement: Shared train-only representation and whole-training costs
All conditional models for a named earlier training population SHALL use its verified global fitted preprocessing, feature order and original feature roles. Local populations SHALL NOT refit preprocessing or rebalance class costs. Balanced per-row costs SHALL derive from the whole earlier population, and unweighted costs SHALL remain one, without duplicate weighting. Original global200 and new global400 controls SHALL retain matching preprocessing and costs; the latter SHALL change only round count. The study SHALL verify exact effective recipes, membership, weight identities, fitted rounds, warnings and persisted inference, and SHALL report actual training/inference work without claiming equal runtime or storage from nominal rounds.

#### Scenario: Train a group with different class prevalence
- **WHEN** a local group's fraud prevalence differs from the whole earlier training population
- **THEN** it retains the whole-population class costs and global representation rather than computing local balanced weights

### Requirement: Canonical fixed score compositions and references
The finite search SHALL retain six original operating references, two global capacity controls, four pure conditional strategies and twelve fixed probability blends, with all 24 canonical identities visible. Blends SHALL use aligned raw probability arithmetic with conditional weights 0.25, 0.5 or 0.75. Zero and unit endpoints SHALL alias the original global and pure conditional identities without extra search entries. Invalid probabilities, nonfinite scores, alignment errors or undeclared arithmetic SHALL fail. Original reference margins SHALL retain their original domains. Role aliases SHALL remain visible while sharing identical work; aliases and tied effects SHALL NOT constitute novel gains.

#### Scenario: Display a unit conditional weight
- **WHEN** a curve includes conditional weight one
- **THEN** it uses the already-declared pure conditional score with no additional fit or renamed strategy

### Requirement: Comparable chronological actual-F2 selection
All compared strategies SHALL predict identical future applications with models and preprocessing fitted strictly earlier. The three operating policies SHALL learn only from eligible earlier out-of-time predictions and labels. Selection SHALL maximize actual macro month-5/6 positive-class F2 beta=2 with deterministic ties, no review quota and no precision floor. Six original operating-reference policies SHALL remain fixed. AP, pooled F2, group outcomes and consumed-month outcomes SHALL NOT replace the selected winner.

#### Scenario: Decide for month six
- **WHEN** a strategy produces month-6 decisions
- **THEN** its model uses months 0-5 and its policy uses eligible month-4/5 predictions and labels only, excluding month-6 labels from model, routing, score composition and rule fitting

### Requirement: Complete comparable effects and group diagnostics
The study SHALL retain every declared strategy, policy, partition, weighting, blend and capacity control, including negative results and ties. It SHALL independently reconstruct the complete declared score, membership, routing, operating, summary and 738 matched-contrast identities. Comparisons SHALL include all six fixed references, own pooled policy, pure/blend versus global200/global400, blends versus pure, higher weights, matched weighting and matched partition. Confusion counts, missed fraud, legitimate flags, precision, recall, F2, flagged fractions, group errors and measured work SHALL accompany results. Empty groups SHALL remain visible. Conditional population changes SHALL NOT be asserted to isolate sample size, representation or total model capacity; group diagnostics SHALL NOT alter the finite search.

#### Scenario: Observe a conditional improvement
- **WHEN** a pure or blended conditional strategy beats its global200 comparator
- **THEN** the same-policy global400, alternate weighting/partition and relevant blend/pure controls remain visible with measured work and any negative effects retained

### Requirement: Frozen bounded exploratory transfer and honest acceptance
Before further consumed-month scoring, the study SHALL freeze the actual winner, six original references, same-strategy pooled policy, predetermined winner-type controls, exact deduplicated dependencies, earlier-only rules, training-only eligibility and actual cache/type-specific fit bounds. Only eight, ten or seventeen predetermined roles SHALL transfer. Losing-grid sweeps, hindsight consumed-month thresholds and winner replacement SHALL be excluded. Month 7 SHALL remain explicitly consumed exploratory transfer without independent-confirmation claims. Broader performance achievement SHALL require positive measured selected-minus-reference F2 differences against all six references on both macro development and consumed transfer. Clear-gain language SHALL additionally require positive conditional interval lower bounds in the named scope.

#### Scenario: Transfer a conditional winner
- **WHEN** a pure or blended conditional strategy wins development selection
- **THEN** its seventeen role identities and exact unique dependencies are frozen before access, and no losing source or weight outside that DAG is scored

#### Scenario: Fail a required reference comparison
- **WHEN** selected F2 fails any required development or consumed comparison
- **THEN** findings retain that failure and the broader performance goal remains unachieved even if the finite study is complete

### Requirement: Independently verified reproducible completion
Completion SHALL require verified outputs, independent reconstruction of source preprocessing, weights, memberships, inference, stitching/arithmetic, chronological policies, masks, metrics, complete factor sets, actual selection and all paired fixed-decision uncertainty draws. Original numerical/resource/persistence gates SHALL remain unchanged; failures and retries SHALL retain evidence. Rendered figures SHALL be checked against values and inspected. Reproduction SHALL include data/software pins, seventeen exact selection restores rejecting changes, individual absent-source instructions, clean-copy methodological tests, actual fit accounting, ordered execution and assistance attribution. Execution/access/source identities and closed-log run/report hashes SHALL be checked before synchronization/archive. Conditional intervals SHALL disclose exclusions for retraining, adaptive source/partition/selection search, temporal/generative variation and multiplicity.

#### Scenario: Fail a declared gate
- **WHEN** the actual host, miniature integration or required source reproduction fails a declared gate
- **THEN** execution retains completed work and failure evidence without relaxing the recipe or claiming completed evaluation

#### Scenario: Finish a negative finite experiment
- **WHEN** every declared output is independently verified but performance comparisons fail
- **THEN** tasks may be marked complete and archived while the negative findings and active broader objective remain explicit
