# baf-bagged-anchor-fusion-study Specification

## Purpose

Evaluate fixed additions of preserved randomized-row fraud ensembles to an existing margin under comparable chronological recall-weighted selection, with independent audits and bounded exploratory transfer.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Prospective isolated preserved study
The study SHALL declare the complete finite strategy, source, arithmetic, policy, reference, destination and verification contracts before evaluating new blends. It SHALL preserve all sixteen completed studies, separate Newton fit/analysis identities and the stopped attempt. Only individually required caches SHALL be inspected. Source reuse SHALL verify exact recipes, eligible training populations, train-only preprocessing, software, numerical validity and saved inference. Wholly absent required sources SHALL reproduce only their exact individual recipes in the new destination under unchanged gates; partial/incompatible inspected caches SHALL fail without overwrite or silent fallback. Unrelated historical grids SHALL NOT be required.

#### Scenario: Encounter an incomplete required cache
- **WHEN** any inspected required model, adapter, prediction or provenance set is partial or incompatible
- **THEN** the study rejects that set without changing it or advancing to another recipe

#### Scenario: Reproduce a wholly absent required source
- **WHEN** all files of a required source are absent and mandatory ancestry remains intact
- **THEN** only that exact individual recipe is reproduced in the new destination, with actual fits counted and historical studies unchanged

### Requirement: Fixed domain-correct label-free addition
Each blend SHALL combine its preserved finite anchor with exactly one declared ensemble margin using a fixed declared weight. Logit of the probability mean SHALL transform the aligned arithmetic probability mean; mean of logits SHALL retain the aligned fixed mean of individually clipped member logits. An existing margin SHALL NOT receive a second probability transform or be constrained to the probability range. Invalid probability domains, nonfinite margins, missing/duplicated members, alignment differences and undeclared arithmetic SHALL be rejected. Current labels, unrelated rows, batch statistics and learned weights SHALL NOT affect a row's composed score. Scores SHALL NOT be asserted calibrated probabilities or operational costs.

#### Scenario: Add an ensemble to an unbounded anchor
- **WHEN** a valid anchor or mean-logit margin lies outside zero to one
- **THEN** it is retained as a finite margin and only probability-like inputs receive the declared clipping and logit transform

#### Scenario: Change current labels or unrelated applications
- **WHEN** a row's source scores are fixed while aligned labels or unrelated rows change
- **THEN** that row's blend score remains unchanged

### Requirement: Canonical controls and visible aliases
Original reference identities SHALL retain their fixed operating policies and reference-winner transfer precedence. Existing canonical logit controls and displayed zero/unit-weight endpoints SHALL retain their established source identities and reconstructible equivalence rather than become renamed new strategies. Visible role aliases SHALL remain in comparisons while sharing identical work. Equivalent decisions and tied effects SHALL NOT be reported as novel improvements.

#### Scenario: Display a unit-weight endpoint
- **WHEN** a blend curve shows weight one
- **THEN** it uses the already-declared ensemble-margin control and does not introduce an additional evaluated strategy or fit

### Requirement: Comparable chronological actual-F2 selection
All strategies SHALL predict identical future applications from models and preprocessing fitted on earlier months. Operating policies SHALL learn only from eligible earlier out-of-time predictions and labels. Selection SHALL maximize actual macro month-5/6 positive-class F2 beta=2 with deterministic ties and no review quota or precision floor. All five original operating references SHALL remain fixed and visible. AP, pooled F2, source diversity or consumed-month outcomes SHALL NOT select a different winner.

#### Scenario: Decide for month 6
- **WHEN** a strategy produces month-6 decisions
- **THEN** its source models use months 0-5, its rule uses eligible month-4/5 predictions and labels only, and composition uses no month-6 labels

### Requirement: Complete matched ensemble and addition effects
Every declared source, representation, weighting, aggregation, blend weight and operating policy SHALL remain visible with the complete fixed-reference, own-policy, blend-versus-anchor/member, blend-weight, matched-weighting/form, representation, constituent and randomized-versus-unbagged comparisons. Ties and negative effects SHALL be retained. Source/decision diversity, clipping, expression provenance and original preprocessing/numerical/resource evidence SHALL accompany results. Otherwise-unmatched changes SHALL NOT be described as isolating one factor; diagnostic errors or correlations SHALL NOT change the finite search.

#### Scenario: Attribute a blend gain
- **WHEN** a blend improves a comparator
- **THEN** its unchanged anchor, added margin, three constituent seeds, alternate weights/form, matched weighting and unbagged controls remain available at the named policy

### Requirement: Frozen bounded transfer and honest acceptance
Before further consumed-month access, the actual winner, five original references, same-strategy pooled rule and predetermined winner-type controls SHALL be frozen with exact deduplicated source/score dependencies, earlier-only rules and actual cache/type-specific fit bounds. Only the declared seven, nine, twenty-one or twenty-six roles SHALL transfer. Losing-grid sweeps, hindsight cutoffs and independent-confirmation claims SHALL be excluded. Broader performance achievement SHALL require positive measured F2 differences against all five references on development and consumed transfer; clear-gain language SHALL additionally require positive conditional interval lower bounds on the named scope.

#### Scenario: Transfer a blend winner
- **WHEN** a new blend wins development selection
- **THEN** its twenty-six roles and exact unique dependencies are frozen before access, and no unselected source or weight outside that dependency set is evaluated

#### Scenario: Fail a required reference comparison
- **WHEN** the frozen selected policy fails any required development or consumed comparison
- **THEN** the findings retain that failure and the broader improvement goal remains unachieved

### Requirement: Independently verified reproducible completion
Completion SHALL require all declared outputs and independent reconstruction of source preprocessing/inference/numerical validity, original aggregations, new compositions, earlier-only policies, masks, metrics, complete factor identities, actual winner and paired fixed-decision uncertainty. Failed numerical/resource/persistence gates SHALL retain evidence without outcome-driven relaxation. Figures SHALL be inspected against underlying values. Reproduction SHALL include pinned dependencies/data, sixteen exact selection restores rejecting changes, individual absent-source reproduction, ordered execution and assistance attribution. Original execution/test/access/source identities and closed-log run/report hashes SHALL be checked before synchronization/archive. Conditional row intervals SHALL explicitly exclude training-seed/retraining, adaptive search/selection, temporal/generative and multiplicity uncertainty.

#### Scenario: Fail an exact required source gate
- **WHEN** a required reproduction fails its preserved numerical, resource or persistence gate
- **THEN** completed work and failure evidence remain intact, with no alternative recipe or completed-study claim

#### Scenario: Complete a negative finite experiment
- **WHEN** all declared work finishes without satisfying the required performance comparisons
- **THEN** verified tasks may complete while the negative result and active broader objective remain explicit
